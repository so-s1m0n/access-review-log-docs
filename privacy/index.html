Privacy policy

Draft only. Not effective or ready for public use. Seller identity, contact, effective date, and final deletion behavior require owner/legal confirmation.

Information processed

The app reads the open Jira project's identity/name and configured permission scheme using the current user's Jira permissions. A saved review includes project ID, key and name, scheme name, configured grant snapshot, decision, optional note, reviewer Atlassian account ID, and timestamp.

Purpose, storage and retention

The app maintains a dated project review register. Records are stored in Atlassian-hosted Forge KVS scoped to the app installation, for up to 365 days and at most 100 records per project. Project admins can delete saved history from the project page. Uninstall deletion behavior is pending live verification. No developer-controlled copy or external data egress is configured.

Permissions and security

The app reads Jira project details and permission schemes. It does not change Jira permissions, read issue content, expand group membership, or calculate effective user access. Reviewer IDs and grant holder identifiers may be personal or confidential information. Avoid entering unnecessary sensitive information in notes.
