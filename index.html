JIRA PROJECT ADMINISTRATION

Access Review Log

Documentation for recording dated decisions about the permission grants configured for a Jira project.

Pre-launch documentation. The app is not listed yet. Contact, legal, and operational details must be completed before launch.

Product documentation

Open the app from a Jira project's settings sidebar. Inspect the assigned permission scheme, record a review decision, and revisit or export the project's history. The app reports configured grants; it does not calculate each person's effective access.
