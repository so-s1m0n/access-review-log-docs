FAQ and troubleshooting

Does this show every person's effective access?

No. It shows configured permission grants. Groups and project roles are not expanded into their members.

Why can't I load a permission scheme?

The current Jira user needs permission to view that project's settings and assigned permission scheme. A 403 or 404 is shown as an error and is not treated as a clean review.

Does the app change permissions?

No. It requests read-only Jira scopes.

Where is history stored?

In app-installation scoped Atlassian Forge storage for up to 365 days, with a 100-record per-project cap. Delete it from the project page.

Preview mode

A trial or active subscription is required to read or save review history. The UI may still inspect current configured grants.
